About the Tournament

Student-Run. Community-Driven.
Problem-Solving.

The TKA Math Tournament (TKAMT) is a local, student-run math tournament for middle school students, hosted by The King's Academy in Sunnyvale, CA. What began as a small community event has grown into one of the most anticipated middle school math competitions in the Bay Area.

The tournament is intended for students in grades 6–8, though younger students are also welcome. Schools may register up to six teams per school, with each team consisting of 3 or 4 students. Teams may mix grades — students don't have to be from the same grade, but they must be from the same school.

We especially encourage schools with newer or less established competition math communities to attend. All participants must attend through their school — non-school, for-profit organizations are not permitted.

Competition Format

Three Rounds, One Champion

TKAMT is structured as a half-day invitational. Three distinct rounds test individual knowledge, team strategy, and real-time speed.

01

Individual Round

50 minutes · 20 short-answer questions. Questions range across all standard topics — algebra, geometry, number theory, and counting & probability. Scored individually; top 3 per grade receive awards.

Individual50 min20 questions
02

Guts Round

1 hour · 8 sets of 3 problems. Teams work together and submit each set before receiving the next. Go fast or take your time — strategy is key. Top 3 teams receive awards.

Team1 hour8 × 3 problems
03

Jeopardy Countdown Round

Teams buzz in to answer and earn points for correct answers, with deductions for incorrect ones. Features Daily Doubles and a Final Jeopardy. High-energy and crowd-pleasing. 1st place team receives an award.
