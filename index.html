About the Tournament

Where Students Compete,
Grow, and Inspire

The TKA Math Tournament (TKAMT) is an annual invitational mathematics competition hosted by The King's Academy. Now entering its second decade, TKAMT brings together top mathematical minds from high schools across the region for a day of rigorous and rewarding problem-solving.

Designed to challenge students across all levels, the tournament features individual sprint and target rounds, collaborative team rounds, and an exciting countdown relay. Whether you're a seasoned competitor or a first-timer, TKAMT offers a welcoming and electrifying atmosphere.

Competition Format

What to Expect

TKAMT is structured as a full-day invitational. Teams of up to six students compete across four distinct events designed to test speed, accuracy, and collaboration.

⚡

Sprint Round

30 individual problems in 30 minutes. No calculators. Tests breadth of knowledge across all major math topics.

Individual30 min
🎯

Target Round

4 pairs of problems with 6 minutes per pair. Emphasis on multi-step problem solving and depth.

Individual24 min
🤝

Team Round

10 problems solved collaboratively by a team of 4–6. Communication and strategy are just as important as math skills.

Team20 min
🔁

Relay Round

Chains of 3-student problems where each answer feeds the next. Mistakes cascade — precision under pressure is key.

Team3 sets
🏁

Countdown Round

Top scorers face off head-to-head on a buzzer system. Fast-paced, high-energy, and crowd-pleasing.

Top 10Elimination

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

TKAMT is open to any high school student in grades 9–12. Each school may register up to two full teams of 6 students, plus additional individual competitors. There are no geographic restrictions — schools from anywhere are welcome to attend.

Registration for TKAMT 2027 will open in Fall 2026. Fill out the interest form below and we'll email you the moment registration opens. Spots fill up fast — last year we reached capacity within two weeks of opening.

Yes. The registration fee is $[X] per team (covers up to 6 students). Individual competitors may register for $[X] per person. Fee details and payment instructions will be sent upon registration confirmation. Financial hardship waivers are available — contact us to inquire.

TKAMT problems span the standard AMC/MATHCOUNTS curriculum: Algebra, Geometry, Number Theory, Combinatorics, and Probability. Difficulty ranges from accessible (AMC 10 level) to challenging (AIME level). No calculus is required. A full topic outline is published on the Resources page each fall.

Calculators are not permitted in the Sprint, Target, or Countdown Rounds. The Team Round allows scientific (non-CAS) calculators. All answers in calculator-free rounds are exact integers or simple fractions.

TKAMT is held on The King's Academy campus. The address and detailed arrival/parking instructions will be sent to all registered teams. The venue is fully accessible and provides competition rooms, a common area for students, and a seating area for coaches and parents.

Yes! Coaches and parents are welcome to attend. There is a designated lounge area with light refreshments. During the Countdown Round, everyone is invited to watch — it's the highlight of the day. The Awards Ceremony is open to all attendees.

Students should bring: multiple sharpened pencils, an eraser, a school-issued ID or signed permission slip, and their own lunch (or money for the on-site concessions). Scratch paper is provided. Students should wear comfortable clothing — they'll be seated for several hours.

Team scores are calculated as a weighted sum of all events: Sprint (40%), Target (20%), Team Round (20%), and Relay (20%). Individual standings are ranked by Sprint + Target scores combined. Ties are broken by the number of correct answers in tiebreaker problems.

Email us at tkamt@tka.edu. We aim to respond within 48 hours. For urgent matters closer to tournament day, a phone contact will be provided to registered teams.
