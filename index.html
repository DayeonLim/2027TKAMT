About the Tournament

Student-Run.
Community-First.

The TKA Math Tournament (TKAMT) is a local, student-run math competition for middle school students, hosted by The King's Academy in Sunnyvale, CA. What began as a small community event has grown into one of the most anticipated middle school math competitions in the Bay Area.

The tournament is designed for students in grades 6–8, though younger students are also welcome. Schools may send up to six teams of 4 students each. Teams may mix grades — students don't have to be from the same grade, but they must be from the same school.

We especially encourage schools with newer or less established competition math communities to attend. All participants must come through their school — non-school, for-profit organizations may not enter teams.

Competition Format

Three Rounds

Three distinct rounds test individual knowledge, team strategy, and real-time speed under pressure.

01

Individual Round

50 minutes · 20 short-answer questions across all standard topics — algebra, geometry, number theory, and counting & probability. Scored individually; top 3 per grade receive awards.

Individual 50 min 20 questions
02

Guts Round

1 hour · 8 sets of 3 problems. Teams work together and submit each set before receiving the next. Go fast or take your time — the strategy is yours. Top 3 teams receive awards.

Team 1 hour 8 × 3 problems
03

Jeopardy Countdown

Teams buzz in to earn points for correct answers and lose points for wrong ones. Features Daily Doubles and a Final Jeopardy. High-energy, live-audience, crowd-pleasing finale.

Team Buzz-in Final Jeopardy
